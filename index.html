Open your save

Pick the save backup zip the launcher made
(menu → Back up your save, saved in the launcher's save backups folder)

You can also drop the zip here, or open the "characters and accounts.sql" file on its own.
Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

After editing

  1. Press Download edited save at the bottom.
  2. Close the game, then in the launcher choose Restore a backup… and pick the downloaded zip.
  3. The launcher backs up your current save first, so you can always go back.
